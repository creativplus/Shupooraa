How Shupora Works

What makes Shupora different from others?

We don't just sell products; we offer solutions. Shupora was founded on the idea of "Smart Curation." We use technology to help you find the *exact* premium wellness tools you need to "Live Better," so you never have to guess what works.

What kind of solutions we offer?

We focus on high-quality, innovative wellness and personal care products. Our curated collection includes advanced hair & scalp care (like our Hard Water Filter and LED Combs), modern posture support, and tools for optimized sleep and recovery.

Do we offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?

Yes, we offer Cash on Delivery (COD) as a convenient payment option for most serviceable pin codes across India. You can select the COD option during checkout to pay for your order in cash when it arrives at your doorstep.

What is your shipping & return policy?

We offer fast, reliable shipping on all orders. If you're not 100% satisfied with your purchase, we have a simple, hassle-free 15-day return policy. You can read our full policy here.
